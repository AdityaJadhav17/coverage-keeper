### Privacy notice: per-state content

One notice can meet all of these. The consumer health data policy below must stay a separate page.

- **All:** name Anthropic and Vercel as processors; state Anthropic's 30-day retention and the flagged-content exception; mention the one-minute IP hold and Vercel Analytics; never claim "HIPAA compliant"; say the site is for adults. [Federal T11](federal.md#topic-11-consumer-protection), [Federal T14](federal.md#topic-14-other), [Federal T12](federal.md#topic-12-children)
- **California (CalOPPA, comply anyway):** categories of personal information and third parties; how users learn of changes; effective date; how we respond to "do not track"; whether others track users across sites. [CA T3](ca.md#topic-3-general-consumer-privacy)
- **Nevada (NRS 603A.340, best practice):** the same five items. [NV T3](nv.md#topic-3-general-consumer-privacy)
- **Oregon (if the OCPA applies):** categories including sensitive data, purposes, rights and appeal, third-party categories, a monitored email, our legal name as registered, how to submit requests. Publish it in Spanish too. [OR T3](or.md#topic-3-general-consumer-privacy), [OR T10](or.md#topic-10-language-access)

### Consumer health data privacy policy

- **Washington (RCW 19.373.020):** data categories and purposes, sources, data shared, third-party categories, how to use the rights. Separate link on the homepage and every page that collects data; only what the Act requires. [WA T2](wa.md#topic-2-health-data-privacy)
- **Nevada (NRS 603A.495):** every item in 603A.495(1)(a)-(k), linked conspicuously from the main site. [NV T2](nv.md#topic-2-health-data-privacy)

### Breach notice deadlines

- **Washington:** people within 30 days; AG within 30 days above 500 residents. [WA T4](wa.md#topic-4-data-breach-notification)
- **Oregon:** people within 45 days; AG above 250. Vendors tell us within 10 days. [OR T4](or.md#topic-4-data-breach-notification)
- **California:** people within 30 calendar days with the set headings; AG sample copy within 15 days above 500. [CA T4](ca.md#topic-4-data-breach-notification)
- **Nevada:** "the most expedient time possible"; credit bureaus above 1,000; substitute notice by website and statewide media. [NV T4](nv.md#topic-4-data-breach-notification)
- **Arizona:** within 45 days; credit bureaus, AG and Department of Homeland Security above 1,000. [AZ T4](az.md#topic-4-data-breach-notification)
- **Federal (if the FTC rule applies):** people, the FTC and, above 500 residents of a state, the media. Take the timing from 16 CFR 318.4, which we did not open. [Federal T4](federal.md#topic-4-data-breach-notification)

### Banned terms ([#50](https://github.com/AdityaJadhav17/coverage-keeper/issues/50))

Never use these to describe Coverage Keeper, its staff or its output, in English or Spanish:

- **Immigration and legal:** notario, notario público, licenciado, immigration consultant, consultor de inmigración, immigration assistant, immigration specialist, abogado, lawyer, licensed, legal aid, legal services, paralegal. [WA T8](wa.md#topic-8-immigration-consultant-laws), [CA T8](ca.md#topic-8-immigration-consultant-laws), [NV T8](nv.md#topic-8-immigration-consultant-laws), [AZ T8](az.md#topic-8-immigration-consultant-laws)
- **Benefits help:** navigator, assister, certified application counselor, enrollment counselor, enrollment facilitator, Community Partner, certified. [Federal T6](federal.md#topic-6-medicaid-and-benefits-assistance), [OR T6](or.md#topic-6-medicaid-and-benefits-assistance), [CA T6](ca.md#topic-6-medicaid-and-benefits-assistance), [NV T6](nv.md#topic-6-medicaid-and-benefits-assistance), [AZ T6](az.md#topic-6-medicaid-and-benefits-assistance)
- **Health professions:** doctor, Dr. (in the product name), nurse, counselor, consejero, therapist, case worker. [CA T5](ca.md#topic-5-ai-specific-laws), [NV T5](nv.md#topic-5-ai-specific-laws), [OR T14](or.md#topic-14-other)
- **Claims:** HIPAA compliant, accurate, guaranteed, or calling Coverage Keeper itself official. [Federal T11](federal.md#topic-11-consumer-protection)
- **Names:** "Federal", "United States" or "National" in the organization or site name; "Medi-Cal" in the product name or domain. [Federal T7](federal.md#topic-7-government-affiliation), [CA T7](ca.md#topic-7-government-affiliation)

The doctor-note template addresses "Dr. [doctor's name]" as a salutation. That is fine; the rule covers how we describe ourselves.
