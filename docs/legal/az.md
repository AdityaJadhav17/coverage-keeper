# Arizona (AHCCCS)

Last reviewed: 2026-11-08. All sources retrieved 2026-11-08 unless marked blocked. Statute text is from azleg.gov. Bill status is from the Legislature's bill-status API (apps.azleg.gov), which feeds its BillStatus pages.

## Summary

- **Arizona's immigration law bars anyone unauthorized from giving "any legal advice" on an immigration matter, paid or not (A.R.S. §12-2702(B)).** The definition of "immigration or naturalization matter" is broad enough to reach a Medicaid letter about non-citizen eligibility. Our explanation must stay general information. See [Topic 8](#topic-8-immigration-consultant-laws).
- **The practice-of-law line (Ariz. R. Sup. Ct. 31) turns on applying law to a person's facts.** The per-person screening result and the doctor note are the parts a lawyer should check. Our only copy of Rule 31 is an unofficial mirror, so its wording is **Unconfirmed**. See [Topic 1](#topic-1-unauthorized-practice-of-law).
- **The breach law (A.R.S. §18-551, §18-552) lists the data our letters carry,** but a breach needs a "database". We keep none, so it likely does not reach us. Anthropic's 30-day retention is the open point. See [Topic 4](#topic-4-data-breach-notification).
- **Arizona has no comprehensive privacy, health-data or AI law in force.** HB 2311 (conversational AI) was vetoed on 2026-06-19. SB 1815 (privacy) and HB 2737 (chatbot data) died in committee.
- **Arizona no longer has charitable solicitation registration.** Title 44, ch. 18 holds only §44-6551 and §44-6561. The anti-deception rules in §44-6561 apply once we solicit. See [Topic 13](#topic-13-charitable-solicitation).
- **No statute reserves the word "AHCCCS".** Don't use the AHCCCS logo or the state seal, and say we are not part of AHCCCS. See [Topic 7](#topic-7-government-affiliation).

## Details

### Topic 1: Unauthorized practice of law

- **Rule 31(b)** (from the [courtrules.net mirror](https://www.courtrules.net/arizona/arizona-supreme-court/rule-31), **Unconfirmed** against the official text) defines practice as "providing legal advice or services to or for another", including preparing a document "intended to affect or secure a specific person's or entity's legal rights."
- The [State Bar](https://www.azbar.org/for-the-public/concerns-about-your-legal-professional/unauthorized-practice-of-law-upl/unauthorized-practice-of-law-upl/) says legal advice "results from the application of the law to facts specific to a person's situation." The State Bar page still cites the pre-2021 numbering, Rule 31(a)(2)(A).
- **Rule 31.3** ([mirror](https://www.courtrules.net/arizona/arizona-supreme-court/rule-31-3), **Unconfirmed**) has no exception for nonprofits, self-help websites or legal information.
- **Opt-in programs we don't need:** legal paraprofessionals ([ACJA §7-210](https://www.azcourts.gov/Portals/0/0/admcode/pdfcurrentcode/7-210%20Legal%20Paraprofessional%20Amended%2010-2025.pdf), amended 10-2025; [program page](https://www.azcourts.gov/cld/Legal-Paraprofessional)), [community justice workers](https://www.azcourts.gov/cld/Community-Justice-Workers) (ACJA §7-211, who work under attorney supervision, including on public benefits) and [alternative business structures](https://www.azcourts.gov/cld/Alternative-Business-Structure) (ACJA §7-209). We read the program pages only; the §7-209 and §7-211 code text returned 404.

**What we must do:**

- Keep every output general: what the letter says, what the program's list says, "ask AHCCCS".
- Never say a person qualifies or which option to pick.
- Show a "not a lawyer, not legal advice" line in English and Spanish.
- Ask a lawyer whether the doctor note and the per-person screening result are information.

### Topic 4: Data breach notification

- A name plus a "health insurance identification number" or "information about an individual's medical or mental health treatment or diagnosis by a health care professional" is personal information ([§18-551(7), (11)(e)-(f)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/18/00551.htm)).
- A breach needs data "maintained as part of a database of personal information regarding multiple individuals" (§18-551(1)(a)). The duty falls on whoever "owns, maintains or licenses" it ([§18-552(A)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/18/00552.htm)). We keep no database. An IP address alone is not a listed element.
- **Open point:** Anthropic may keep inputs up to 30 days. That could be a database maintained for us. A maintainer must notify the owner (§18-552(C)).
- Notice is excused if a breach is not reasonably likely to cause substantial economic loss (§18-552(J)). HIPAA entities are exempt (§18-552(N)(2)); we are not one. Only the AG enforces, capped at $500,000 per breach (§18-552(L)).

**What we must do:**

- Keep the no-storage design.
- Put breach cooperation in the Anthropic contract, or get ZDR.
- Keep a short incident plan. If notice is owed: within 45 days after determining a breach. Above 1,000 people, also notify the three largest credit bureaus, the AG and the Arizona Department of Homeland Security (§18-552(B); [AG page](https://www.azag.gov/consumer/data-breach)).

### Topic 6: Medicaid and benefits assistance

The [AHCCCS Community Partner - Assistor Organization page](https://www.azahcccs.gov/Resources/CommunityPartners/HEAplus.html) asks groups to "Submit a HEAplus Community Partner Application form". AHCCCS may deny one in its sole discretion. The page does not bar non-partners from helping. A no-fee commitment reportedly sits in the CP-100 form, which we did not open. **Unconfirmed.**

**What we must do:** never call ourselves an "AHCCCS Community Assistor" or "Community Partner". If we later want HEAplus access or a referral listing, apply and expect no-fee and training terms.

### Topic 7: Government affiliation

- [§13-2406](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/13/02406.htm) needs pretending to be a public servant to make someone submit to "pretended official authority". We don't.
- [ACOM Policy 425](https://www.azahcccs.gov/shared/Downloads/ACOM/PolicyFiles/400/425_Social_Networking.pdf) binds AHCCCS health-plan contractors, not us. It still shows the agency's position: "The use of the AHCCCS logo is prohibited unless prior approval has been received." It also bars the state seal.
- We found no statute reserving "AHCCCS" or requiring a disclaimer. We did not search USPTO. **Unconfirmed.**
- A look-alike site could be deceptive under [§44-1522](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/44/01522.htm) if the Consumer Fraud Act reaches us (Topic 11).

**What we must do:**

- Use "AHCCCS" only to name the program.
- No AHCCCS logo, state seal, or look-alike colors or layout.
- Add: "Coverage Keeper is run by [nonprofit]. It is not part of AHCCCS or the State of Arizona."
- Link only to official azahcccs.gov and keepmyahcccs.com pages and numbers.

### Topic 8: Immigration consultant laws

- [§12-2701(1)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/12/02701.htm): an "immigration or naturalization matter" includes all matters implicating any law related to a person's immigration or citizenship status. A Medicaid letter about non-citizen eligibility may fit.
- [§12-2702(B)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/12/02702.htm): "no other person or persons may represent others in any case, prepare applications or forms or give any legal advice relating to any immigration or naturalization matter." This is not limited to paid help. §12-2702(C) bars misrepresenting one's immigration services.
- [§12-2703(A)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/12/02703.htm) separately bars such services "for compensation", and makes violations a class 6 felony. We are free.
- [§12-2704](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/12/02704.htm) makes violations Consumer Fraud Act violations.
- [§41-273(C)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/41/00273.htm) bars non-attorney notaries from "notario" or "notario publico". It reaches us only if we advertise as notaries. The old cite, §41-328, returns 404.

**What we must do:**

- For immigration-status letters, explain only what the letter says and what AHCCCS asks for.
- Never advise on status, visas, green cards, public charge or USCIS filings. Never fill USCIS forms.
- Add in both languages: "We can't give immigration advice. Talk to an immigration lawyer or a DOJ-accredited representative."
- Never use "notario" or "notario público".

### Topic 9: Accessibility

[§41-1492.02(A)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/41/01492-02.htm) bars disability discrimination in "any place of public accommodation". The definitions in [§41-1492](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/41/01492.htm) copy ADA Title III and list physical places, including a "social service center establishment". The text says nothing about websites. We found no Arizona court opinion on websites.

**What we must do:** meet WCAG 2.1 AA (or 2.2 AA): alt text, keyboard use, contrast, screen-reader labels, plain language. Provide a way to report access problems.

### Topic 11: Consumer protection

[§44-1521](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/44/01521.htm): "merchandise" includes services, but "Sale" means a sale or attempt to sell "for any consideration" (§44-1521(7)). A free tool likely isn't a sale or advertisement. Donations or partner money could change that. The immigration law (§12-2704) and breach law (§18-552(L)) make their violations unlawful practices under [§44-1522](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/44/01522.htm) regardless.

**What we must do:** make every claim literally true:

- "we don't store your letter" must reflect Anthropic's 30-day retention
- "no cookies" must hold
- say AI can make mistakes
- say the Spanish is machine-assisted until reviewed
- keep the privacy notice in sync with the code

### Topic 12: Children

We found no Arizona children's online privacy statute in force. HB 2311 had minor-account rules but was [vetoed](https://apps.azleg.gov/api/Bill/?billNumber=HB2311&sessionId=130). The AHCCCS work rule covers ages 19 to 64 (`data/rules/az.json`), and we have no accounts. We did not search all Arizona statutes, so the negative is **Unconfirmed**.

**What we must do:** nothing for Arizona. See [federal.md](federal.md#topic-12-children) for COPPA.

### Topic 13: Charitable solicitation

The [Title 44 index](https://www.azleg.gov/arsDetail/?title=44) lists the charitable-solicitation chapter as §44-6551 to §44-6561, with two sections. §44-6552 returns 404. When we solicit, [§44-6561](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/44/06561.htm) bars misleading names, false claims of sponsorship or endorsement, and false tax-deduction claims. §44-6561(A)(4) bars claiming another person "sponsors, endorses or approves the solicitation" without written consent. The AG may fine up to $1,000 per violation. We inferred the repeal from the chapter's contents; we did not confirm its date.

**What we must do:**

- Don't claim AHCCCS or any agency endorses us.
- Don't say gifts are tax-deductible until the IRS 501(c)(3) letter exists.
- Have a lawyer confirm when registration was repealed.

### Start-point corrections

- **Immigration consultants:** A.R.S. §12-2701 to §12-2704 are correct and current.
- **Notary advertising:** §41-328 returns 404. The current rule is §41-273.
- **UPL:** the definition is now Rule 31(b), effective Jan. 1, 2021, per the unofficial mirror. Add ACJA §7-211 (community justice workers).
- **Breach law:** the article runs §18-551 to §18-552.
- **Consumer Fraud Act:** the unlawful-practice rule is §44-1522.
- **AHCCCS assister rules:** the program is the Community Partner - Assistor Organization (CP-AO) program, a contract, not a statute.
- **Charitable solicitation:** no registration statute remains.

## Questions for a lawyer

1. Under Rule 31(b), is a per-person screening result ("This may apply to you. Ask AHCCCS about it.") legal information and not legal advice?
2. Is the doctor note a "document ... intended to affect or secure a specific person's ... legal rights" under Rule 31(b)? If so, does generic, unfilled wording fix it?
3. Is explaining a Medicaid letter about non-citizen eligibility an "immigration or naturalization matter" under A.R.S. §12-2701(1)? Would our disclaimer keep us outside §12-2702(B)?
4. Does Anthropic's 30-day retention make it a party that "maintains" a "database" of our users' data under §18-551 and §18-552? Do we then "own or license" it and owe notice?
5. Is a free service with optional donations a "sale" or "advertisement" of "merchandise" under §44-1521?
6. Does the Arizonans with Disabilities Act (§41-1492.02) reach a web-only nonprofit service?
7. Is "AHCCCS" a registered mark? Does AHCCCS need to approve our naming of the program?
8. When did Arizona repeal charitable-organization registration?
