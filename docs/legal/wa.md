# Washington (Apple Health)

Last reviewed: 2026-11-08. All sources retrieved 2026-11-08 from app.leg.wa.gov, courts.wa.gov, atg.wa.gov, hca.wa.gov and lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov. No page was blocked.

## Summary

- **The My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) likely covers us now.** It has no nonprofit exemption. "Collect" includes "receive" and "otherwise process", so "we store nothing" does not take us out. We need a separate consumer health data privacy policy linked from the homepage, a request and appeal process, and a binding processor contract with Anthropic. Violations count as Consumer Protection Act violations. See [Topic 2](#topic-2-health-data-privacy).
- **Consent may not be needed for the core feature.** The Act allows collecting and sharing data "to the extent necessary to provide a product or service that the consumer ... has requested." Vercel Analytics and Anthropic's 30-day retention are the weak points. A lawyer decides.
- **Unauthorized practice of law is a crime here (RCW 2.48.180).** GR 24 protects general information, but its general definition covers applying law to another person's circumstances. The screening result is the closest call. See [Topic 1](#topic-1-unauthorized-practice-of-law).
- **The immigration law (RCW 19.154) bans titles like "notario" whether or not we charge.** Its conduct bans need pay and an immigration proceeding, so they likely miss us. See [Topic 8](#topic-8-immigration-consultant-laws).
- **The breach law (RCW 19.255) likely applies.** Letters show a name with a health insurance ID. We need a 30-day notice plan. See [Topic 4](#topic-4-data-breach-notification).
- **HCA guidance governs the "Apple Health" name.** Don't use the logo without permission. Call the program "Washington Apple Health (Medicaid)" the first time. See [Topic 7](#topic-7-government-affiliation).

## Details

### Topic 1: Unauthorized practice of law

[GR 24](https://www.courts.wa.gov/court_rules/pdf/GR/GA_GR_24_00_00.pdf) (amended effective September 1, 2025) defines the practice of law.

- GR 24(d) protects "information of a general nature about the law and legal procedures to members of the public."
- GR 24(a)(1) covers advice "for fees or other consideration". We charge nothing.
- The general definition in GR 24(a) is broader. It covers applying legal principles "with regard to the circumstances or objectives of another". Summarizing one person's letter and showing "This may apply to you" sits near that line.
- GR 24(a)(2) covers selecting or completing legal documents. The doctor note is a letter to a doctor, not a legal document. A lawyer should confirm.

[RCW 2.48.180](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=2.48.180) makes unlawful practice a gross misdemeanor. Repeat violations are a class C felony.

**What we must do:**

- Quote what the letter says. Never tell the person their legal rights or what to do in an appeal or hearing.
- Keep the "Ask Apple Health" handoff.
- Add: "General information, not legal advice. For help with your case, contact a lawyer or Washington legal aid."
- Never select or fill in answers on HCA forms. Never represent anyone at a fair hearing.

### Topic 2: Health data privacy

Source: [RCW 19.373](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.373&full=true), read in full, and the [AG FAQ](https://www.atg.wa.gov/protecting-washingtonians-personal-health-data-and-privacy).

**Why it covers us:**

- **No nonprofit exemption.** A "regulated entity" (.010(23)) is "any legal entity" that targets Washington consumers and decides why and how data is processed. The only entity carve-out covers government agencies, tribes and their contractors. We are not an HCA contractor.
- **Small business.** We likely fit .010(28) (under 100,000 consumers a year). That status only delayed the start date, which passed on June 30, 2024. All duties still apply.
- **Consumer health data.** .010(8) includes data that identifies "a consumer seeking health care services" and health conditions and treatment. Letters and screening touch Medicaid coverage, medically frail status, pregnancy, substance-use treatment and inpatient stays.
- **IP addresses count** as personal information (.010(18)).
- **Pass-through counts.** "Collect" (.010(5)) includes "receive ... or otherwise process ... in any manner". Sending photos through our server to Anthropic is collection.
- **Screening answers** that never leave the browser are likely not collected by us. A lawyer should confirm.
- **Exemptions (.100) don't help.** We hold no HIPAA PHI, and we are not the health benefit exchange.

**Consent.** .030(1)(a)(ii) and (b)(ii) allow collecting and sharing without consent "to the extent necessary to provide a product or service that the consumer ... has requested." Disclosure to a processor for the original purpose is not "sharing" (.010(27)(b)(i)).

**Processors.** .060(1) lets a processor handle data "only pursuant to a binding contract" with instructions and limits. A processor that goes beyond them becomes a regulated entity for that data. Anthropic's 30-day retention for its own safety purposes needs review against this rule.

**Vercel Analytics.** Page views tied to an IP on a Medicaid site could identify "a consumer seeking health care services". AG FAQ Q6 says inferred data counts when used to link a person to a health status. A lawyer decides.

**What we must do:**

1. Publish a separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy covering the .020(1)(a)(i)-(v) items: data categories and purposes, sources, data shared, third-party categories (Anthropic, Vercel), and how to use the .040 rights.
2. Link it on its own from the homepage and from every page that collects data. "Homepage" (.010(16)) includes any page where personal information is collected. AG FAQ Q4 says the link "must be a separate and distinct link", and the policy may hold only what the Act requires.
3. Set up a request channel with no account needed (.040). Answer within 45 days (one 45-day extension). Offer an appeal and point to the AG on denial. Pass deletion requests to Anthropic. Answer truthfully even when we hold nothing.
4. Sign a binding processor contract with Anthropic, and with Vercel if its data is covered (.060). Check the 30-day retention fits, or choose ZDR.
5. Get affirmative consent before any new purpose, such as training or partner sharing (.020(1)(c)-(d), .030(1)(b)(i)).
6. Keep reasonable security and limit staff access (.050). Never sell data (.070). Don't geofence (.080).

**Confidence:** high that the Act covers a nonprofit like us. Medium on consent, Vercel and Anthropic.

### Topic 4: Data breach notification

[RCW 19.255.005](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.255.005) and [19.255.010](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.255.010):

- "Personal information" is a name plus a listed element. Two appear on Medicaid letters: a health insurance ID number (.005(2)(a)(i)(G)) and medical history or condition (H).
- Duties fall on anyone who "owns or licenses" the data. A breach involves data "maintained by the person or business". Whether data held only in memory is "maintained" is for a lawyer.
- A vendor that maintains data it doesn't own must tell the owner immediately (.010(2)). That would be Anthropic.
- Notice is not required if harm is not reasonably likely.

**What we must do:**

- Write an incident plan. Notify affected Washington residents "no more than thirty calendar days after the breach was discovered" (.010(8)), in plain language with the required contents (.010(6)). Notify the AG within 30 days if more than 500 residents are affected (.010(7)).
- Add breach-notice clauses to the Anthropic and Vercel contracts.
- Encrypt in transit.

### Topic 5: AI-specific laws

[ESHB 2225](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Biennium/2025-26/Pdf/Bills/Session%20Laws/House/2225-S.SL.pdf) (ch. 168, Laws of 2026) takes effect January 1, 2027. Its "AI companion chatbot" must be able to sustain a relationship across interactions (sec. 2(1)(a)). Sec. 2(1)(b)(i) excludes bots used for customer service or "productivity and analysis related to source information". The [House Bill Report](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Biennium/2025-26/Htm/Bill%20Reports/House/2225-S.E%20HBR%20PL%2026.htm) matches. Coverage Keeper has no accounts or memory and reads documents.

HB 1170 (AI content provenance) did not open. Its scope is **Unconfirmed**.

**What we must do:** don't add chat memory, a persona or open-ended chat without a new review. Label output "written by AI" for consumer-protection reasons (Topic 11).

### Topic 6: Medicaid and benefits assistance

[RCW 43.71.010](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=43.71&full=true) defines a navigator as "a person or entity certified by the exchange". Navigator duties in .075 bind only certified navigators. We are not certified and we don't touch Washington Healthplanfinder. We found no Washington ban on payment for Medicaid help in the statutes we opened.

**What we must do:** never call ourselves a "navigator", "assister" or "in-person assister". Send application help to HCA, Healthplanfinder and certified navigators. Review again before partnering with a navigator organization.

### Topic 7: Government affiliation

No statute bans using the "Apple Health" name. HCA guidance sets the rules:

- The [H.R. 1 Communications Toolkit](https://www.hca.wa.gov/assets/free-or-low-cost/hr1-communications-toolkit.pdf) (updated Oct 7, 2026) says to call the program "Washington Apple Health (Medicaid)" or "Apple Health (Medicaid)" the first time, then "Apple Health". It says not to create logos that differ from the official one, and to request the logo from ahcommunications@hca.wa.gov.
- The [HCA design standards, 60-0004](https://inside.hca.wa.gov/sites/default/files/60-0004-hca-design-standards-omnibus.pdf), are internal and not binding on us. They add a caveat that clients must meet eligibility rules whenever coverage is called "free or low-cost".

A look-alike site could be deceptive under [RCW 19.86.020](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.86&full=true). Criminal impersonation ([RCW 9A.60.040](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=9A.60.040)) needs intent to defraud and does not fit. Whether "Apple Health" is a registered trademark is **Unconfirmed**.

**What we must do:**

- No Apple Health logo, HCA logo or state seal without HCA's written permission.
- Use HCA's naming format.
- Add to the header or footer and to each result: "Coverage Keeper is run by [nonprofit]. We are not part of the Washington State Health Care Authority or Apple Health."
- Link to official hca.wa.gov pages.
- Never call coverage "free" without the eligibility caveat.

### Topic 8: Immigration consultant laws

[RCW 19.154](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.154&full=true), the Immigration Services Fraud Prevention Act:

- **Conduct bans** (.060(1)-(2)), such as advising on status, apply only "for compensation" and only in an "immigration matter". An immigration matter (.020(2)) is a proceeding or filing affecting immigration or citizenship status. A Medicaid letter is not one, and we charge nothing.
- **Title bans** (.060(3)) apply "regardless of whether compensation is sought". No one may hold out, on a website or in any language, as a "notario publico, notario, immigration assistant, immigration consultant, immigration specialist", or claim to offer immigration services that would be the practice of law.
- **Translation** of a form's words is allowed (.060(4)(b)).
- **Penalties:** CPA liability with a $1,000 minimum (.090) and a gross misdemeanor (.100).
- **Donations** could raise the "anything of value" question later.

**What we must do:**

- Never use those titles, including "notario" and "consultor de inmigración".
- For letters that mention immigration status, describe only what the letter says about Apple Health coverage. Never say what someone's status is, what to seek, or how a Medicaid decision affects an immigration case.
- Add: "We do not give immigration advice. For immigration questions, contact an immigration attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative."
- Make the guardrail block status statements in English and Spanish.

**Confidence:** high for the title ban. Medium that a Medicaid letter is not an "immigration matter".

### Topic 9: Accessibility

[RCW 49.60.215](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=49.60.215) bars any act that "directly or indirectly results in any distinction, restriction, or discrimination" in a place of public accommodation, including for disability. "Person" includes organizations ([RCW 49.60.040](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=49.60.040)). "Place" includes places where the public gathers for public purposes and places offering personal services. The statute does not say whether a free website counts. We did not open Washington case law. **Unconfirmed.**

**What we must do:** meet WCAG 2.1 AA or 2.2 AA. Publish an accessibility statement with a contact. Test photo upload, PDF upload and results with a screen reader.

### Topic 10: Language access

[RCW 74.04.025](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=74.04.025) puts the bilingual-services duty on "the department, the authority, and the office of administrative hearings". It does not reach private nonprofits. We take no HCA funds. Unreviewed Spanish still creates accuracy risk under the CPA.

**What we must do:** get a fluent Spanish review before launch. Label the Spanish as machine-assisted until then. Point people to HCA's free interpreter services.

### Topic 11: Consumer protection

[RCW 19.86](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.86&full=true):

- .020 bans unfair or deceptive acts "in the conduct of any trade or commerce".
- Whether a free nonprofit is in "trade or commerce" for a stand-alone claim is **Unconfirmed**. MHMDA (.090) and RCW 19.154 (.090) make their violations CPA violations regardless.
- Private plaintiffs must show injury to "business or property". Treble damages are capped at $25,000. The AG can seek up to $7,500 per violation (.140), with higher penalties for practices that target people by immigration status or disability.

**What we must do:**

- "We don't store your letter" must say that Anthropic may keep it up to 30 days, unless ZDR is in place.
- "No cookies" must hold for Vercel Analytics too.
- Never imply the tool decides eligibility or is official.
- Show the source line for each deadline and tell users to check it against the letter.

### Topic 12: Children

MHMDA's "consumer" (.010(7)) is any natural person who lives in Washington, with no age limit. Some letters cover children (Apple Health for Kids), and screening asks about age. ESHB 2225 has minor-specific duties for companion chatbots only. We did not search every Washington minors' privacy law. **Unconfirmed.**

**What we must do:** handle children's letters as consumer health data, the same as adults'. See [federal.md](federal.md#topic-12-children) for COPPA.

### Topic 13: Charitable solicitation

[RCW 19.09](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.09&full=true): any entity that solicits contributions from the public for a charitable purpose is a "charitable organization". It must register with the Secretary of State unless it raises under $50,000 a year and everyone working for it is unpaid (.081(1)). Point-of-solicitation disclosures (.100) apply to all solicitors.

**What we must do before taking donations:** register or document the exemption. Name the organization and its city on the donate page. If registered, add the line saying the registration is on file with the Secretary of State.

### Topic 14: Other

- **Doctor note ([RCW 18.71.011](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=18.71.011)), likely does not apply.** Practicing medicine means offering to "diagnose, cure, advise, or prescribe". The template asks the person's own doctor whether they can write a statement. The "medically frail" question repeats HCA's own category. We found no ban on helping a patient ask for a certification. Keep the template neutral, never suggest a diagnosis or the doctor's wording, and say only the doctor decides.
- **Biometrics ([RCW 19.375](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.375&full=true)), likely does not apply.** The definition excludes "a physical or digital photograph", and a letter photo yields no identifier template. Revisit if we add ID or face capture. Confidence high.
- **Criminal impersonation ([RCW 9A.60.040](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=9A.60.040)), does not apply.** It needs intent to defraud. Topic 7 covers look-alike risk.

### Start-point corrections

- **MHMDA:** the private right of action runs through the CPA (RCW 19.373.090, 19.86.090), and plaintiffs must show injury to business or property. Small businesses carry the same duties.
- **GR 24:** last amended effective September 1, 2025. The criminal penalty is in RCW 2.48.180.
- **RCW 19.154:** its official name is the Immigration Services Fraud Prevention Act. "Immigration assistant" is now a banned title, not a registered role.
- **"Apple Health" name:** no statute. HCA guidance and CPA deception rules govern it.

## Questions for a lawyer

1. Is Coverage Keeper a "regulated entity" or "small business" under RCW 19.373, as a free nonprofit that stores nothing?
2. Does passing letter images through our server to Anthropic count as "collecting" consumer health data?
3. Does the "necessary to provide a product or service that the consumer ... has requested" exception (RCW 19.373.030(1)(a)(ii), (b)(ii)) cover our core feature, so that no consent screen is required?
4. Do Anthropic's commercial terms and DPA meet the "binding contract" rule of RCW 19.373.060? Does 30-day retention for safety make Anthropic a regulated entity for that data, so that we need ZDR?
5. Are Vercel Web Analytics page views plus IP addresses "consumer health data" because they identify "a consumer seeking health care services"?
6. Are screening answers that never leave the browser outside the Act?
7. Is a free nonprofit tool in "trade or commerce" for a CPA claim not routed through MHMDA?
8. Under GR 24, is the screening result ("This may apply to you. Ask Apple Health about it.") general information under GR 24(d), or applying law to "the circumstances ... of another" under GR 24(a)?
9. Is explaining a non-citizen Medicaid eligibility letter outside "immigration matter" in RCW 19.154.020(2)?
10. Would accepting donations make our service "for compensation" under RCW 19.154?
11. Is data held only in memory and in transit "maintained" by us under RCW 19.255.005(1)?
12. Is a free informational website a "place of public accommodation" under RCW 49.60.040(2)?
13. Is "Apple Health" a registered trademark? Do we need HCA's permission to use the words, not the logo?
