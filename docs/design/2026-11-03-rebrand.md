# Rebrand: warm, calm, not just Oregon

Date: 2026-11-03 · Owner: Aditya Jadhav · Ships with issues #15 (layout) and #18 (West Coast states) · Story: US-095

## Intent

Coverage Keeper is about to be opened to the public as an NGO project. The people who use it have just received a letter that could cost them their health coverage. The app should feel like **a calm person sitting next to them**, someone who has read a hundred of these letters and says, "Here's what it wants, and you've got time." Today it looks like a government office (navy and grey, icons on everything) and speaks as an Oregon-only tool.

The name stays **Coverage Keeper**.

## Goals

1. A warm, welcoming look (palette A, "evergreen and marigold") that still reads as trustworthy enough for a health-coverage letter.
2. Copy that leads with the person's outcome, says the effort is small, shows the path, and keeps them in control.
3. No state-specific identity in the brand: works for WA, OR, CA, NV and AZ (#18).
4. Looks like a professional product: text-first, few icons, a real logo mark, no emoji.

## Non-goals

- New name, new font for body text, illustrations or photography.
- Any change to how letters are read, the guardrail, or what is stored.
- Marketing site or landing pages beyond the app's own welcome screen.

## Anti-goals (a failure even if it ships)

- Anything that says "you're in trouble" before they read a word: red headers, alarm banners, stern navy.
- Looking like an official government site, or copying any agency's colors or seal.
- Template or generated look: an icon before every line, emoji, gradients, everything centered, identical rounded cards everywhere.
- Childish or salesy warmth. Invented social proof ("10,000 people helped").
- Any contrast below WCAG AA, or urgency shown by color alone.

## Inspirations (and what we take)

- **GetCalFresh** (Code for America): warm off-white, evergreen solid button with outlined secondary, serif headline in plain words, "talk to a real person" near the top, language switch in the header.
- **Propel**: serif headline, rounded pill buttons, soft tinted panels, a short row of trust facts.
- **GOV.UK Design System**: status as labelled text tags, not icons; decorative icons retired.
- Healthcare brands (One Medical, Oscar): a medium-weight serif for headings reads modern and approachable.

Measured contrast (WCAG): ink on bg 13.9 (light) / 15.1 (dark); muted on panel 5.2 / 7.1; primary on bg 7.3 / 9.8; on-primary on primary 7.8 / 9.1; alert on panel 4.6 / 6.8; text on mark 10.3 / 11.2. Form control borders use `--muted` (≥ 3:1).

### Type

- Headings: **Source Serif 4**, 600-700, via `next/font/google`, `letter-spacing: -0.01em`, sentence case.
- Body and UI: **Atkinson Hyperlegible** (unchanged; chosen for low-vision readers), body never below 17px on phones.
- The due date uses the heading serif at 28-32px.

### Icons and marks

- **Logo mark:** rounded square in `--primary` with an off-white letter outline and a marigold check (SVG, `src/app/icon.svg` and the header). It replaces the envelope glyph before the name.
- **Functional icons only:** phone (call bar), camera (capture button). One stroke family, 2px at 20px, `currentColor`. Remove the calendar, clock, help and alert icons from the deadline card.
- **Status = labelled badge:** pill with a small dot and words: "5 days left", "Due today", "Date has passed. Call now." (alert colors), "Not sure. Check the letter." Never color alone.
- No emoji anywhere in UI, copy or docs.

### Shape and spacing

- Buttons are pills (`border-radius: 999px`), 48px tall, primary solid, secondary outlined.
- Group content with tinted `--panel` blocks (radius 18px), not borders. Only the due-date panel and the call bar get emphasis.
- Left-aligned text; nothing centered except the call bar.

## Copy (decided)

Principles: outcome first, effort second, show the path, keep the user in control, only claims we can back up, a human exit on every screen. Sentence case, plain words, grade 6-8.

Welcome screen (replaces today's consent screen; consent stays on it):

> **Got a letter about your health coverage?**
> Take a photo. In about a minute you'll know what it asks for, when it's due, and what to send.
> [ Start with my letter ]
> About a minute · No sign-up · Free
> We don't save your photo. You decide what to send; we never contact the state for you.
> 1 Photo → 2 What it means → 3 What to send
> Rather talk to a person? Call the number on your letter. We show it on every screen.
> _Small print:_ Free and independent. Not run by any state agency. This isn't legal advice; only your state decides your coverage. An AI service reads your photo for us.

"I understand" is replaced by "Start with my letter", which opens the capture screen. Tapping it is the consent: the consent facts (including the small print) sit on the same screen above it, so US-055 still holds.

Result screen: label "{Program} · {State}" above the heading "Here's what your letter says"; sections "Due date", "What the letter asks you to do", "Papers to send".

## Constraints

- **Order of work:** this rebrand (tokens, mark, copy) merges **before** #16 Task 4 moves UI strings into `src/i18n/messages.ts`, so Spanish translates the new copy once. Tell Waleed when it merges.
- Phone layout and flow stay the same apart from colors, type, the welcome copy and the badge (US-094). E2E tests may change only where visible text changes; keep roles and `data-testid`s.
- No new npm dependencies (Source Serif 4 comes through `next/font/google`, already used for Atkinson).
- Dark mode is designed, not inverted, and passes axe in E2E.
- "About a minute" must stay true: the reader's timeout is 30 seconds.

## Calls made after review (mine, check these)

1. **Scope split with #18.** The rebrand changes look, type, icons and copy. Oregon-specific _content_ stays until #18 replaces it: the call bar's fallback OHP number, the screening rules, and the Deadline "apply again" note. Welcome copy avoids naming OHP or Oregon.
2. **No "Check exemptions first" on the welcome screen yet.** It needs #18's state picker (US-081). #18 adds it.
3. **CTA label "Start with my letter"**, not "Read my letter", because #17 uses "Read my letter (N pages)" for the final upload step.
4. **Privacy line is "We don't save your photo."** "Then delete it" overclaims: the photo goes to the AI provider, which keeps API data under its own retention terms (confirm in the launch-readiness work).
5. **Badge wording (fixed):** "{N} days left" / "1 day left", "Due today", "Date has passed. Call now." (alert colors), and for an unknown date "Not sure of the date. Check the letter." The dot is part of the label, not a decorative icon. `NOT_SURE` stays for the other fields.
6. **Tests:** US-094 is amended. E2E tests may change text selectors only where copy changed; roles, labels on form controls, and `data-testid`s stay as the contract.
7. **"About a minute"** is a copy constraint, backed by the reader's 30-second timeout; no new measurement.
