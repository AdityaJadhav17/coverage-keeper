# Personas

All personas assume a low-cost Android phone, poor indoor lighting, a cracked or smudged camera, and a limited data plan. Personas are illustrative composites, not real people.

## P1 (primary): Marisol, gig worker with irregular hours

- **Role:** 34, delivers for two apps and cleans houses. Oregon Health Plan (OHP) member through Medicaid expansion.
- **Goals:** Keep OHP without losing a shift to sit on hold. Know exactly what to send and by when.
- **Frustrations:** Hours swing month to month, so she is unsure if she hits 80 hours or $580. Letters are long and use program codes. She found a letter two weeks after it arrived.
- **Tech comfort:** High on her phone, low on government portals.
- **Language:** Bilingual English/Spanish; prefers Spanish for official topics.
- **Device:** Android, 32 GB, prepaid data.

## P2: Darnell, caregiver for his mother

- **Role:** 52, not working outside the home; cares for his mother with dementia about 30 hours a week plus nights.
- **Goals:** Find out if caregiving counts and what proof the state wants.
- **Frustrations:** Does not know that caregiving may be an exemption. Afraid that saying "I don't work" will cost him coverage.
- **Tech comfort:** Medium. Uses phone for calls, texts, photos.
- **Language:** English.
- **Device:** Older Android, low storage.

## P3: Priya, chronic illness

- **Role:** 41, has lupus with flares that stop her from working some months.
- **Goals:** Get the right statement from her doctor before the deadline.
- **Frustrations:** Doesn't know what the doctor's note must say. Appointments are weeks out, so time matters.
- **Tech comfort:** High.
- **Language:** English.
- **Device:** Mid-range Android.

## P4: Tuan, adult child helping a parent

- **Role:** 29, helps his father (58, Vietnamese speaker, OHP member) with mail.
- **Goals:** Understand his father's letter fast and explain it to him in Vietnamese.
- **Frustrations:** Gets photos of letters over chat; they are often cropped or blurry.
- **Tech comfort:** High.
- **Language:** English for himself, Vietnamese for his father.
- **Device:** Android; works from photos his father sends him.

## v0.2 personas

These cover the four v0.2 features: Spanish, full-letter reading, West Coast states, and laptop layout.

## P5: Rosa, Spanish-only OHP member (Spanish)

- **Role:** 47, Salem, Oregon. Works seasonal shifts at a food-processing plant. Her OHP letters come in Spanish because she chose Spanish as her language.
- **Goals:** Understand her letter without asking her teenage son to translate it.
- **Frustrations:** Many tools are English-only. Translated pages often sound robotic or use words she doesn't use.
- **Tech comfort:** Medium. WhatsApp, Facebook, camera.
- **Language:** Spanish only.
- **Device:** Low-cost Android, prepaid data, phone set to Spanish.

## P6: Kim, community health worker (full-letter reading, laptop)

- **Role:** 36, certified OHP assister at a community clinic. Helps 10-15 people a day.
- **Goals:** Read a client's whole letter quickly, including page 3 where the papers list is, and print or show the result on her laptop.
- **Frustrations:** Clients bring 4-page letters, PDFs downloaded from their online account, or a folder of photos on a USB stick. Re-photographing every page wastes the appointment.
- **Tech comfort:** High on a laptop.
- **Language:** English and Spanish.
- **Device:** Work laptop (Windows, Chrome, 1366x768), sometimes her own iPhone.

## P7: Daniel, Medi-Cal member in California (multi-state)

- **Role:** 39, Fresno. Drives for a delivery app and cares for his daughter (10) half the week.
- **Goals:** Know which California exemptions might apply to him and who to call at his county.
- **Frustrations:** Apps and articles mix up states. He doesn't know if Oregon rules apply to him.
- **Tech comfort:** High on his phone.
- **Language:** English.
- **Device:** iPhone 12.

## P8: Leilani, Apple Health member in Washington (multi-state)

- **Role:** 27, Spokane. Part-time student and barista.
- **Goals:** Check whether her school hours count toward Washington's rule.
- **Frustrations:** Her letter says "Apple Health", not "Medicaid", so search results confuse her.
- **Tech comfort:** High.
- **Language:** English.
- **Device:** Android, often on campus Wi-Fi.

## P9: Gloria, older user on a library computer (laptop layout)

- **Role:** 63, Reno, Nevada. Doesn't own a smartphone with a good camera; uses the public library's computers and scanner.
- **Goals:** Upload a scanned letter and read the result in large text.
- **Frustrations:** Phone-sized layouts on a big screen look broken. Browser zoom at 200% breaks many sites.
- **Tech comfort:** Low to medium. Uses the mouse, sometimes only the keyboard.
- **Language:** English.
- **Device:** Library desktop (Windows, Edge, 1920x1080), browser zoom 150-200%.
